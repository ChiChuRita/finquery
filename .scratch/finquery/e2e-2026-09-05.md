# FinQuery end-to-end test, 2026-09-05

Target: `http://127.0.0.1:8099` (fresh database, `FINQUERY_PROVIDER=openrouter`, Gemma 4 26B fast /
Qwen3.5 9B quality). Browser: headed Chrome, 1440x900 (1024x768 for the width check).
Screenshots: `/tmp/finquery-e2e/NN-*.png`.

## Verdict

FinQuery is close to demo-ready and, for a build this wide, remarkably solid: onboarding, the
sample-year import, the Question cards, the query sub-agent with its guard, seven of eight chart
shapes, changesets with Apply/Discard/Undo/stale, the split editor, duplicates with remove-all, the
PDF statement (433 of 433, reconciled, 171 s), vision on both a synthetic and a real receipt, memory
across chats, context compression with an editable summary, preference pairs with a JSONL export, and
web lookup with its outbound log and cache all worked exactly as their tickets describe, with zero
console errors and zero failed network requests on every page. Three things stop it being a clean run:
pressing **Stop** while a chart is being planned took the whole app down to the error boundary once
(not reproducible on a second try, but a reload was the only way back); the **CSV mapping
confirmation card renders with no buttons at all**, so any non-preset bank import parks unless the
user happens to type a confirmation in prose; and a **bill split card said "Applied" while writing no
legs**, which is the one defect that quietly corrupts what the user believes about their data. Two
more matter for Monday: the **second Question card never appears** after the first is answered (demo
step 5 is dead), and the sentence under a grouped-bar chart described the *previous* chart. Fix those
five and the script in `docs/demo-script.md` runs end to end.

## Real receipt

`fixtures/private/edeka-bill-real.jpeg` (iPhone 16 Pro, 4032x3024, EXIF orientation upper-right)
attached in a chat on `http://127.0.0.1:8100` and sent with "Here is a receipt". 17 s end to end.

**The rotation fix works**: the photo was read upright, not sideways, and nothing was refused.

| Field | Read by the app | On the paper | Match |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Merchant | EDEKA Müller | EDEKA Müller, Marlene-Dietrich-Allee 15, 14482 Potsdam | yes |
| Total | -4,73 € | SUMME € 4,73 | **yes** |
| Line items | 3 (Uludag Gazoz 0,33l -0,99, Pfand -0,26, Hi-Chew Original -3,49) | 3 ("Posten: 3"): 0,99 B, 0,25*B, 3,49 A | count yes, Pfand off by 1 cent (0,26 vs 0,25) |
| Date | not read, fell back to today (05.09.2026) | Datum Uhrzeit 04.09.26 20:00 | **no** |

Because the one-cent error made the items miss the printed total, the card said so honestly: "The line
items do not add up to the printed total. The date on the receipt could not be read." No synthetic
booking matched, so a **new transaction preview** appeared ("Add this transaction? EDEKA Müller, Cash,
05.09.2026, -4,73 €") with Confirm and Discard, exactly as ticket 11 asks. Confirm wrote the row and
categorized it: `05.09.2026 | EDEKA Müller | EDEKA | -4,73 € | Groceries > Supermarket | Cash | Manual`.

Two small findings, neither a blocker: the **date on the receipt was not read** (the app used today
instead of 04.09.2026, and the date is printed plainly under the barcode), and the **Pfand line was
read as 0,26 instead of 0,25**, which is what broke the sum check. The total itself, the merchant and
the item count were right. Screenshots 76 (card), 77 (confirmed), 78 (row), and `real-receipt-view.jpg`
for the paper.

## Console and network

Zero console messages and zero 4xx/5xx responses on `/`, `/transactions`, `/import`, `/memory`,
`/feedback`, `/settings` and on a conversation carrying eight chart iframes. The only two errors seen
all run were the Stop crash (B1) and the A/B 500 (M2), both listed above.

## Top fixes before Monday

1. **B2, the mapping card.** It is one missing `rows`/`options` hand-off and it kills every bank that
   is not one of the six presets. Rendering the card's own `options` when `rows` is empty would be
   enough, plus a guard so a card with nothing to answer is never emitted.
2. **B3, the split that said Applied and wrote nothing.** A card lying about the database is worse
   than a card that fails. Make `apply` fail loudly, and re-read the parent's `split_count` before
   flipping the badge.
3. **M1, the second Question card.** Demo step 5 is in the script. `review_batch` returning a queue
   must be followed by an `ask_user` card, or the step should not claim there is a card below.
4. **B1, the Stop crash.** Guard the `.length` read on a half-arrived chart data part. Stop is on the
   script (step 16) and the audience sees the error boundary if it fires.
5. **M4 and M3, the chart answers.** The prose under a chart must come from that chart's rows, and the
   stacked-bar SQL has to group by both columns. Both are visible in the first two minutes of the
   chart section of the demo.
